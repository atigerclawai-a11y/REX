## 12. The CDPAP FI Purge — How HFC Lost Its Revenue Pipeline

### Background: The Single FI Mandate
The **2024-25 NYS Budget** required all ~280,000 CDPAP Medicaid recipients to switch from their existing Fiscal Intermediary (FI) — one of approximately **600 FIs** — to a single statewide FI: **Public Partnerships LLC (PPL)**. The transition deadline was April 1, 2025.

**Home Family Care was one of those ~600 FIs.** Its website (hfcny.com) explicitly advertised CDPAP services. As a CDPAP FI, HFC earned revenue on every PA it managed — payroll processing, benefits administration, and a per-member administrative fee from Medicaid.

### What HFC Lost
| Revenue Stream | Status |
|---|---|
| **CDPAP FI revenue** (HFC as FI for its CDPAP consumers) | **ELIMINATED** (April 2025) |
| **LHCSA home care services** (non-CDPAP) | Still operational |
| **MLTC plan contracts** (non-CDPAP) | Still operational |
| **Private pay clients** | Unknown |
| **Home infusion therapy** (Home Infusion Group) | Unknown |

### CDPAP was HFC's Primary Offering
HFC advertised CDPAP prominently on hfcny.com. Per the Fiscal Policy Institute, most CDPAP FIs did NOT offer health insurance to their PAs — meaning the FI model was a low-overhead administrative revenue stream. The loss of CDPAP likely represented a substantial revenue cut for HFC.
