## 6. KNOWN WEAKNESSES

- Blue-ink checkmarks are invisible to focr (reads names fine, returns empty `marks: {}`)
- focr produces garbage abbreviations: Вин (Винегрет), Кур (Куриный суп), Свкл (Свекла), MP (Пюре), FF (Картошка фри), Гр (Гречка)
- Surya garbles handwritten names (e.g., "Pako Mayya" for "Рако Маууа")
- Surya produces shorthand tokens (Котл. кур, Свкл, Б) that fail the dish catalog contract gate
- Tilted/unpaired pages silently dropped by the page-pairing reader — 24 pages across 7 batches were never read
- `generate_tomorrow.py` day-name resolution is date-relative: after midnight, `--day Tuesday` resolves to NEXT week
- `goj_kitchen_paired.py` `JOIN clients` silently drops clients missing from the proprietary `clients` table
- `generate_tomorrow.py draw_kitchen()` caps salads/soups at 6 rows and combos at 10, hiding dishes
- `CC_unified_sheets.py` reads from ghs_schedule.db (RETIRED), not auth_tracker — produces wrong attendance counts
- `CC_drive_preflight.py` embedded preflight() zeroes all `day_*_actual` when Drive is unreachable
- `generate_tomorrow.py` requires Python 3.10+ (uses `|` union syntax) but system `python3` is 3.9.6
- Proprietary DB sync is one-way (REX→Documents) — Documents-only writes are silently reverted within 5 minutes
- Cross-shift duplicates (client has both S1 and S2 rows for same date) break shift-fix UPDATEs
- Partial OCR rows (exists but empty cells) defeat the fill chain — client silently drops off kitchen sheets
- Carried-forward history can re-infect current week with abbreviations if history isn't canonicalized

### Fuzzy Match Locations (where a person can silently become two)

1. **name_alias table** — `alias → roster_name` mapping, difflib-based. Unmatched OCR names hit this.
2. **`_norm()` function** in `generate_distribution_sheet.py` — strips punctuation, lowercases. Used for menu↔attendance matching.
3. **Surname-fragment matching** — `last_name_fragment IN roster_name` over-matches (Safonov→Safonova). Risk of carrying a relative's order.
4. **Carecenta↔auth name reconciliation** — reverse name order, spelling variants (Mariia/Maria, Nataliya/Natalia, Katerskaia/Katerskaya).
5. **Cyrillic↔Latin transliteration** — in OCR output, y↔i, ks↔x variants create duplicate client entries.
