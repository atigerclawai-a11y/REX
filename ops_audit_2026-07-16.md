### 1.2 Victoria Bot — 3 Critical Bugs

Victoria makes automated outbound attendance confirmation calls to GOJ clients in Russian. She is **operational but degraded** — ~51% confirmation rate on 98-call samples.

| Bug | Evidence | Impact |
|-----|----------|--------|
| **Wrong name** | Greets EVERY client as "Allen" regardless of `client_name` dynamic variable. Confirmed on Lysenko, Margulis, and Allen calls. | Client confusion, distrust, hang-ups. Ukrainian-speaking client asked "Are you in Ukraine?" after the mismatch. |
| **Stale date** | References "July 2" on July 16 calls. July 11 calls referenced "July 12." | Clients get incorrect attendance date, cannot confirm properly. |
| **No voicemail detection** | Loops "I don't understand" against voicemail prompts for 90s+. 66% of disconnects are user_hangup. | Wastes Retell call credits on dead air. Confirmed on Lysenko call (90s voicemail loop). |

**Estimated cost:** At ~50+ calls/day with 66% user_hangup rate and ~15-90s durations, retell billing is likely $30-60/day in wasted credits from these bugs.

**Root cause:** Victoria's Retell LLM prompt has dynamically-named variables that aren't being populated at call time — `{{client_name}}` resolves to the literal string "Allen" and `{{date}}` resolves to a hardcoded "July 2." These are Retell Dashboard configuration issues, not code bugs.

### 2.2 Masha-BBG QA Pipeline — CRITICAL

**Status:** HALTED — 46 consecutive prompt-bug deadlocks  
**Wasted:** ~$1,668  
**Duration:** Since late June (~3 weeks)  
**Root cause:** Masha's Retell base prompt (`llm_13da395e8bab02945fe497bbd1f7`) hardcodes her identity as "you are Masha, the warm and friendly receptionist at Boardwalk Beer Garden" and her `begin_message` as "Hello, this is Masha from Boardwalk Beer Garden." When Masha calls Lana, both bots think they're the BBG receptionist, triggering a reciprocal politeness loop (40-304 seconds of mutual thanking, zero substantive exchange).

**Fix:** 30 seconds in Retell Dashboard. Change Masha-BBG `general_prompt` IDENTITY from "receptionist at BBG" to "customer calling BBG." Change `begin_message` from "this is Masha from Boardwalk Beer Garden" to "Hi, I'm calling about..."

**Pipeline correctly self-halted** to prevent further waste. The LLM backup file (`CC_masha_llm_backup.json`) confirms the prompt is unchanged since `last_modification_timestamp: 1782232203437` (late June).
